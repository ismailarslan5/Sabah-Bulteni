https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-09/google-escalates-nvidia-rivalry-with-new-versions-of-its-tpu-systems

https://venturebeat.com/technology/meituan-releases-the-open-weights-of-its-longcat-2-5-llm-with-vision-under-a-permissive-mit-license

https://www.forbes.com/sites/annatong/2026/11/09/fireworks-cyberattack-exposes-customer-data-and-ai-models-as-fallout-widens/
